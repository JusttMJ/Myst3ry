# Myst3ry: video demonstration script

The brief asks for a **short** video of the running app, with your voices explaining what you are doing. It must show that the app meets **all** the requirements. **Don't explain the code** (that goes in the PDF). Aim for about 4–5 minutes.

## Before recording

- Record the Android emulator (or a phone) with a screen recorder, e.g. the Windows **Snipping Tool** video mode or OBS. Save as **MP4** at about **720p** so the text is readable but the file stays small.
- Use a microphone and a quiet room. Split the sections below between group members so everyone speaks.
- On the **Stats** tab, tap **Clear History**. Then uninstall and reinstall the app, or clear its storage, so that the first-launch help appears.
- Rehearse a game once so you know roughly how to reach a win in a few guesses.

## Script

| # | Show on screen | Say (roughly) | Requirement covered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home screen, then tap the padlock icon; splash screen | "This is Myst3ry, our .NET MAUI code-breaking game." | App name / visual design |
| 2 | How to Play opens by itself; scroll through it | "On first launch the rules open automatically: the app picks a secret three-digit number with all digits different. Hits are right digit, right place; matches are right digit, wrong place." | Explains the game |
| 3 | Tap **Got it**; point at each part of the Play screen | "Here's the difficulty selector, the timer, guess count and best score, the guess history, the slots and the keypad." | UI overview |
| 4 | Normal mode: type a digit, show it greyed out; show 0 disabled at the start; use ⌫ | "Digits can't repeat, so used digits grey out, and the code can't start with 0. The clock starts on the first tap." | Input, timer |
| 5 | Submit 3–4 guesses; point at the hits/matches labels and status hint | "After each guess we're told the hits and matches, for example one hit and two matches." | Hits and matches feedback |
| 6 | Re-enter a guess you already tried | "Repeating a guess is rejected and not counted." | Usability |
| 7 | Keep guessing until 3 hits; result card | "Three hits wins. The app shows that it took N guesses, the time, and the code." | Win at 3 hits; number of guesses shown |
| 8 | Tap **Play Again** | "We can play again straight away, as many times as we like." | Play repeatedly |
| 9 | Switch to **Easy**; make 2 guesses | "Easy colours each digit: green hit, amber match, grey not in the code." | Difficulty levels |
| 10 | Switch to **Hard** (confirm dialog if guesses were made); let the clock reach under 10 s | "Hard gives 60 seconds; the clock turns red at the end and the game is lost at zero." | Timer |
| 11 | New game: make a guess, then tap **Give Up** and confirm | "The player can also quit; the code is revealed." | Quit the game |
| 12 | **Stats** tab; scroll; show **Clear History** confirmation and cancel it | "Every game is saved on the phone: totals, win rate, best, average, fastest and recent games." | Extra functionality |
| 13 | Switch the emulator to dark mode and show the Play screen | "The app also supports dark mode." | Visual design |
| 14 | Back to the Play screen | "That's Myst3ry. Thanks for watching." | |

## After recording

- Watch it back and check that the text on screen is readable and the audio is clear.
- Zip the MP4 together with the final PDF for submission.
